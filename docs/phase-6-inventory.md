# Phase 6 inventory contract

Implemented October 30, 2026 for synthetic local observations and real PostgreSQL/Exasol. Gate execution is recorded in the [Phase 6 handoff](handoffs/phase-6.md). Phase 7 and advertising approval/execution remain outside this module.

## Requirements and evidence

Accepted requirements are exact available/reserve arithmetic, observed exposure, uncertain sparse/zero demand and unknown lead times, weighted size availability, time-aware replenishment, inventory freshness, restricted recovery, reviewed mapping/capability scope, pinned nullable finance, explicit approval for every advertising mutation, INR 0 and no live integration. Numerical policies below are labelled implementation defaults, not merchant-confirmed settings or calibrated forecasts.

The [source models and policy](../backend/advergent/inventory.py) accept one complete observation per pinned commerce variant at warehouse-1. The deterministic [generator](../backend/advergent/inventory_fixture.py) creates [inventory-v1.json](../fixtures/inventory-v1.json). This is an independent simulated warehouse observation source: available stock, already-excluded commitments, separate reserve, observed eligible fulfilled-unit counters and measured in-stock seconds over a stated window. These counters are not extra commerce orders, sales, financial revenue or attribution events. Existing commerce catalog/variant identity and historical orders remain unchanged. Most initial stock values reproduce the commerce mirror; v6 explicitly observes 40 units. New simulated observations are labelled; replay does not create a new observation. This is not live warehouse verification or evidence of complete real demand history.

The warehouse counters represent units observed during measured in-stock exposure, not demand inferred from today's availability. This prototype accepts aggregate source counters; it does not reconstruct continuous availability from current stock, infer lost sales, seasonality, cancellations or promotions, or claim to estimate uncensored true demand. Missing exposure remains null even if a unit counter exists. Sources must supply counters consistent with the declared window. Unknown real-world coverage is not repaired by fixtures.

## Arithmetic and assumptions

Exasol computes sellable units = max(0, available - separate merchant reserve). Committed units remain visible but are never subtracted again. Inventory does not add expected receipts or returned units to available stock; a later explicit warehouse count must observe sellable recovery.

Default window is 28 days. Variant daily velocity = eligible units * 86,400 / observed in-stock seconds. The default evidence floor is ten units and seven observed in-stock days per variant. Cover is stock / velocity only with sufficient positive-demand evidence. Sparse demand, zero demand and missing/zero exposure leave decision cover null; no infinity or implied safe stock results. Raw observed rate remains visible as evidence, with its sufficiency status. A product's reliable demand and cover require sufficient evidence for every key variant; one uncertain size cannot silently disappear from the denominator.

All six catalog sizes are key variants in this single-color fixture. Product demand = sum of reliable variant daily rates; product cover = total sellable stock / that rate. Weighted unavailable size share is the sum of rates for unavailable sizes / total rates. This corrects different exposure durations rather than comparing raw sales counts. Complete explicit merchant weights can supply a labelled assumption fallback when demand rates are insufficient. Partial/all-zero fallback weights cannot resolve uncertainty. Exasol calculates the product sums, cover and weighted shares; policy comparisons use decimal values without display rounding. Variant cover and size risk remain visible because aggregate cover alone can conceal an empty key size.

Lead times are complete per-product settings with explicit source, allowing null for unknown. The default is an unconfirmed synthetic 14-day configuration assumption; p5 is unknown. Default safety buffer is three days. Warning means a reliable variant's cover is below lead plus safety. Unknown lead remains uncertain and prevents recovery eligibility; independent observed stockout/low-cover evidence can still justify protective advice.

A09: v6 observes 40 available units, three already-excluded commitments, zero separate reserve and 280 units over 28 in-stock days: ten/day and four-day variant cover, with 14-day lead time. It produces approval-required product throttle advice. Whether an exact advertising throttle is eligible depends separately on reviewed mapping and budget control. A10: p0's unavailable first three sizes carry 60% of observed demand weight; supporting sizes and counters are exposed. Product-level advice never asserts a supported product-level platform mutation.

## Replenishment and freshness

Receipts retain identity, variant, quantity, expected time, confirmation status, source and observation time. Walk confirmed fresh receipts in expected-time order from the stock observation, extending depletion cover by quantity / rate. A receipt after an uncovered gap never bridges that earlier gap. Unconfirmed, cancelled, overdue and stale receipts do not suppress protection. An exact depletion-boundary arrival is an explicit optimistic simulator convention. It is not a logistics guarantee. Default throttle is a reliable variant below five days with no receipt sequence bridging through lead plus safety; a token quantity cannot suppress it. Receipts remain evidence, never current stock.

Default stock/resource/receipt freshness limit is 900 seconds, inclusive, evaluated against actual UTC query time. Scenario time cannot bypass production eligibility. Individual affected variants and exact resource observations must be fresh; overall snapshot freshness reports the oldest stock observation. Reimport preserves both source observation and original analytical completion time. Historical default source observations remain stale and block eligibility for their affected products. Old finance/commerce cutoffs stay visible in their pinned views; a new warehouse observation does not refresh those sources.

## Protective and recovery advice

Pause condition is zero total sellable stock, or unavailable key-size weight at least 60%. Definitive zero stock does not require invented positive demand. Default recovery stock conditions are positive stock, at least seven days reliable product cover, unavailable weight strictly below 40%, known lead, sufficient demand and no pause/throttle condition. A warning alone is advisory; pause, throttle, uncertainty, stale observations and explicit external blockers prevent recovery. A zero-stock reliable variant also creates a throttle blocker even below the product size-risk threshold.

The exact simulated resource must currently be paused, have paused_by=AdVergent and an explicit prior pause evidence ID. Merchant/unknown pauses and absent prior system evidence cannot recover. Imported pause evidence is a synthetic resource observation, not an implemented action ledger or proof that AdVergent performed an actual mutation. Phase 8 must replace this with durable approved action history and execution-time revalidation. Other supplied blockers must clear. An active resource never receives a recovery recommendation.

Mapping is resolved again at evaluation time using the pinned Phase 4 evidence: half-open validity, confidence floor, override precedence and ambiguity abstention. Native hints never become mappings; no parent inheritance is invented. Each recommendation exposes exact platform/account/resource, reviewed product set, mapping evidence, resource state, budget identity/members, capabilities, blockers and approval-required/mutation-disabled status. All product evidence and assumptions are accessible in the same snapshot.

A pause needs exact supported status control and every product in the complete reviewed resource set to qualify for pause. A throttle needs every affected product to qualify, exact resource budget ownership/control and an unshared scope. An unavailable product alongside a healthy or merely low-cover product never authorizes a broad mixed-resource pause. Unsupported narrow controls yield advisory results (A11). No product control is invented, shared budget is not silently changed, and no percentage/amount is executed. Product-level throttle/pause advice is advisory until exact supported resource scope can be established. Any eligible result is still only approval-required advice; this module has no approval or execution endpoint.

## Finance impact and ownership

Inventory pins one finance dataset, which pins commerce, ads and first-party attribution. Impact is the pinned historical purchased-product expected contribution before ads (after remaining return reserve), across uniquely assigned and unattributed orders; it is contextual exposure, not a future lost-profit estimate, avoided spend, causal lift or incremental profit. All matching lines count once in each independent recommendation context; contexts overlap and must not be summed. Missing necessary costs make full context contribution null. A labelled covered-only subtotal and incomplete-line count remain visible. No missing cost becomes zero in a full estimate. Product selection follows purchased variants, not platform claims. Negative known contribution remains negative. A separate daily contribution scenario applies each product's historical expected contribution per delivered unit to its observed warehouse velocity, with delivered-unit sample size and the formula exposed. Historical failed-order costs remain included. Missing product costs, absent delivered history or insufficient demand leave this scenario null. It is an uncalibrated assumption across differing source cutoffs, not a demand-response forecast, guaranteed loss, incremental profit or claimed model effectiveness.

[Migration 006](../migrations/006_inventory.sql) stores immutable full configuration (including simulated resource/pause evidence), pinned finance identity, attempts and independent publication in PostgreSQL. Commerce/ad/attribution/finance/inventory advisory locks pin inputs through publication. A new import requires current finance to match current upstream IDs. Inventory identity uses namespace inventory-v1:, exact source SHA-256 and finance ID. Configuration changes use trusted complete-source CLI imports, with no browser editor.

The [Exasol adapter](../backend/advergent/inventory_sql.py) retains typed stock/demand/exposure/weights, full source payloads and a completion marker with configuration and finance identity. SQL calculates actual features and independently reconciles stock. Full source/core/configuration/dependency verification precedes PostgreSQL publication. SQL failure rolls back observation rows and marker; verification failure preserves prior publication. Commit-before-publication retry verifies/reuses the immutable orphan. There is no cross-store transaction or cleanup guarantee.

Authenticated GET /api/inventory and the [UI](../frontend/src/Inventory.tsx) expose stock, rates, nullable cover, sizes/receipts/lead evidence, suggested actions, exact-scope eligibility, historical financial context, assumptions, source/hash/input/query/publication timestamps and upstream-change warnings. Changed upstream pointers retain the old view and block eligibility. Actual analytics failure suppresses all current stock/features/recommendations/query time while retaining publication evidence. Operational source snapshots never become an analytics fallback.

## Commands and limits

Load private environment with `. .\scripts\env.ps1`, then run `.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m advergent.inventory_fixture`, `worker migrate` and `worker inventory` using the same Python executable. `worker inventory --fixture PATH` selects another complete immutable source. [Handoff](handoffs/phase-6.md) records exact isolated/real-store/browser commands and actual outage restoration.

No paid provisioning, live inventory/platform integration, advertising write, Phase 7 diagnostics or Phase 8 approval/execution exists. Aggregated synthetic counters, all-six-size policy, uncalibrated thresholds/lead times, optimistic exact-boundary receipt timing, bounded source capacity and imported simulated pause provenance are explicit limitations. Production interval reconstruction, merchant calibration, live completeness, actual action ledger, large-scale performance, cleanup and OS hard-kill/reboot recovery remain unverified.
