# Phase 12 budget optimization contract

Authorized and verified October 31, 2026 at the synthetic local gate; exact evidence is in the [handoff](handoffs/phase-12.md). A21: an attractive stockout product must never receive growth; fixed INR daily total and per-budget change limits must hold. This module is read-only. No proposal, approval, publishing, simulator initialization or advertising write is performed. Existing exact explicit approval and execution revalidation remain mandatory in Phase 8.

Accepted requirements: conservative response baselines, fixed total, change bounds, inventory precedence, uncertainty and abstention. Implementation defaults: 10% maximum change (configurable downward), integer paise; existing portfolio total is the fixed budget. A different total is infeasible rather than silently changing funding. Each distinct budget appears once, including all shared members. Blocked resources freeze at their baseline; retaining an unsafe baseline is an abstention, never an endorsement or substitute for protective inventory review.

Response baseline: descriptive spend/contribution association, assuming allocated daily budget is fully spent. It is not a causal response, calibrated LTV, guaranteed return, confidence interval or incremental profit. Use complete 48-half-hour India days for every additive reporting leaf of the whole budget, unambiguous campaign-to-budget ownership, and the same day's attributed placed-order contribution observed for 60 days. All orders (including failed deliveries) count; actual dated expense/refund/recovery events within that horizon count once, including pre-placement order expenses. Unknown costs or corrections unavailable by the exclusive end make the day unknown. No cash, reserves, platform claims or funnel value enters. Require at least three positive spend levels, three days per level, twenty orders per day, complete costs and mature windows. Days must be unique and contiguous. Estimated cost lines remain explicitly counted in observations. These strict uncalibrated floors intentionally abstain on the bundled sparse reporting history. Missing or incomplete days cannot be selectively dropped into a curve.

For each adjacent spend-level pair, bound the descriptive slope by (minimum higher-level contribution minus maximum lower-level contribution)/spend difference and (maximum higher minus minimum lower)/difference. The envelope over all adjacent pairs is displayed as exact rational strings. Negative/non-monotone lower slopes abstain. No extrapolation beyond observed spend support. Within support, allocate equal integer paise from a donor only if a recipient's lower slope exceeds the donor's upper slope; maximize this separable conservative delta using descending positive marginal differences and deterministic identity ties. The fixed spend total cancels the after-ad spend delta. Report delta, not an invented portfolio contribution total; unknown baseline contribution stays unknown. Ranges are descriptive scenario sensitivity, not probabilistic bounds.

Every affected member needs exact current reviewed mapping, supported budget control, active observed status and clear fresh inventory for every mapped product; shared scope cannot bypass a blocked member. Fresh ads/inventory/diagnostics, resource states and destination/catalog checks gate all reallocations. Missing costs, unknown demand/lead, stale/future evidence, ambiguous attribution scope and insufficient response support freeze the resource. Current historical product finance remains separately nullable: `historical_contribution_paise` is the pinned expected contribution before ads after remaining return reserve, a separate context field that never trains the actual-event response baseline. Product scopes may overlap across resources; do not sum this context. Source-age and mapping boundaries are checked again after analytical work. Six coherent analytical pins and read-only simulator state/revision, kill switch and unknown-intent snapshots must remain unchanged during a query. No data or timestamp survives analytics failure; no PostgreSQL analytical fallback.

Existing cohort 60/90-day mature/immature counts, missing windows, incomplete history and cutoff are returned as separate context. Cohort totals never become a response multiplier; prospective budget response uses all attributed order outcomes over its own explicit mature 60-day horizon. Retrospective corrected source snapshots cannot establish historical knowledge or a predictive backtest. Historical stock exposure, prices, promotions and tracking completeness are not reconstructed; model effectiveness is unverified.

Authenticated GET `/api/budget` uses defaults; origin-protected POST `/api/budget/scenario` accepts only fixed total and a 0–1000 basis-point bound. Terms are ephemeral. Exasol verifies existing typed/source cores, joins day/budget reporting and order/campaign evidence, and calculates dated financial contribution. Python owns vendor-neutral baselines/allocation. PostgreSQL owns existing state only; no schema, importer, worker, source fixture, publication pointer or saved recommendation is introduced. INR 0. Phase 13 needs another requested chat.
