# Phase 11 cohort contract

Authorized October 31, 2026. A20 requires mature and immature contribution to remain separate without future leakage. Accepted metric rules: earliest delivered purchase in available history, explicit incomplete history, and fractional first-purchase product assignment by net merchandise value summing to one. This is synthetic local descriptive management reporting, not calibrated LTV, incremental profit or an advertising decision.

Implementation defaults: exact rolling half-open 60/90-day UTC intervals starting at the earliest delivery timestamp from finance evidence; equal timestamps break by lexical order ID. Acquisition month is Asia/Kolkata. First-purchase weights use quantity times historical unit price less allocated discount, excluding tax, shipping and later refunds. Multiple variants of one product combine. Exact rational numerator/denominator preserves one customer; all-zero first merchandise abstains rather than inventing value weights. No new cohort publication, fixture orders, operational schema or action interface.

Reuse finance's exact pinned commerce, ads and first-party attribution IDs. Exasol verifies upstream typed/source evidence and joins customers, actual purchased products and unique first-party assignments; it calculates dated event contribution. The observation cutoff is the minimum of finance as-of and all required source observations, capped by actual UTC time. Optional aware `as_of` can only move this cutoff earlier. Historical source versions are retrospective evidence, not proof of what was known operationally at that time. This is not a point-in-time predictive backtest.

For each customer include actual events from acquisition delivery through the exclusive window end/cutoff. Include acquisition-order costs incurred before delivery, once, so delivery cannot erase acquisition expenses. Other pre-acquisition events are excluded and counted. Repeat orders, failed deliveries, refunds and recovery assessments retain actual event time and remain assigned to that customer's original cohort. Post-window refunds are excluded, not moved backward; recovered value uses its assessment effective time. Cash, reserves, platform claims, breakout events and ad spend never become cohort contribution. Values are realized before ads; repeat orders do not create new customers. Expenses for later orders follow their effective event date. Maturity means the entire window is observed, not that every return has completed.

Missing event costs propagate null contribution; covered event subtotal is separately labelled and is neither a full estimate nor a bound. Estimated evidence stays labelled. Any historical correction effective after the query cutoff or at/after the exclusive window end makes affected delivery contribution unknown rather than leaking a future cost. Future events cannot alter weights or earlier window totals. Each known customer total allocates to first products using largest-remainder integer paise with lexical product ties; negative totals reconcile too. Exact customer fractions remain rational strings, not rounded basis points. Mature and immature product/month buckets are separate, including partially mature months. Unknown customers make affected full bucket contribution null; known customer subtotals remain explicit. Per-customer and product allocations reconcile.

The bounded commerce snapshot has no verified lifetime/older-order coverage declaration: every assignment is explicitly first observed, history incomplete. `period_start` is the finance interval boundary, not proof of exhaustive merchant history. The population is customers with recorded orders; ordering customers without an observed delivery and zero-value unassigned customers remain counted. Customers without any recorded order are outside this purchase-cohort population. Changed or concurrently changed pins suppress current results; analytics outage removes all results without a PostgreSQL analytical fallback. All publishing/mutation flags remain false. No Phase 12, paid API, provisioning, billing change or action history cleanup; INR 0.
